Wireless Speaker Delay Measurement

Usage Instructions:

This tool plays a high-frequency sound to measure the delay of your wireless speaker. Please set your computer's sound output to the wireless speaker.

When prompted, grant microphone access. The test runs continuously: it plays short beeps at the tone frequency entered above (you can change it while the test runs - the next beep uses the new value), listens for each beep through the microphone, and reports per-beep and median delay. The first beep is a warm-up and is excluded from the median. Press "Stop Test" when you have enough samples, then "Use This Delay" to save the median.

Important for Bluetooth headsets: make the built-in microphone (not the headset microphone) the default input device before measuring. A headset mic forces the HFP call profile and adds its own uplink delay, which inflates the result.

For best results, place your computer as close as possible to the speaker and keep the surrounding environment quiet.

If the measurement fails, you can manually set the delay in the extension popup.
